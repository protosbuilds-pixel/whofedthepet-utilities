4. Third-Party Services

Backend & Data Storage: Supabase (Supabase Inc.; data processed in Dublin, Ireland / EU data center). Stores account data, pet data, feeding and medication records, household sharing information, and reminder settings. A Data Processing Agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place.

Email Delivery (account confirmation, password reset): Resend (Resend, Inc., USA), integrated as a custom SMTP provider through Supabase Auth. Transmitted data: email address, name, content of system emails. Data transfer to the United States is based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision) and additionally the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).

Reminders / Notifications: Reminders are scheduled locally on the user's device only (Expo Local Notifications). No data is transmitted to any push notification server, and no push notification tokens are collected or stored.

Payment Processing: Apple Inc. (App Store / In-App Purchase) for iOS devices, and Google Ireland Ltd. (Google Play Billing) for Android devices. Both providers act as independent controllers for payment processing; their respective privacy policies apply in addition to this Policy.

Error Tracking: Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.; data processed in Frankfurt, Germany / EU data center). Processes technical device information and error reports to improve application stability.

5. Data Storage and Retention

  • Crash and error reports (Sentry): retained for up to 90 days.
  • Subscription and transaction records: retained as required by applicable tax and accounting law (in Germany: up to 10 years pursuant to § 147 AO).
  • Reminder settings: stored locally on the device; removed when the app is uninstalled.

Users may request deletion of their account and associated data at any time by contacting protosbuilds@gmail.com.
